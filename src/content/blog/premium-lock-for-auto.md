Eventual Climate launched Premium Lock for Auto on October 21, 2026, extending the multi-year pricing guarantee from homes to cars. Here is what that means for Kansas City drivers.

## What changed

Premium Lock started with homeowners, and the people who used it asked for the same predictability on the other big recurring bill: the car. The answer arrived this week. Premium Lock for Auto is live, available in all 50 states with any admitted lines carrier, on 6- or 12-month policies, creating 3 years of pricing predictability.

## Why it matters now

The average American already pays more to insure their car than their home, which makes auto an overlooked but significant financial stress. Home insurance is often hidden inside the mortgage escrow payment; auto is the visible, frequent bill that gets re-shopped and serviced every term. That is exactly where renewal surprises hurt most.

As Dylan DiMarchi, Eventual's CEO, put it at launch: "The multi-year nature of Premium Lock handily resolves most auto retention problems for our distribution partners." For you, that means price-sensitive drivers get clarity on their future expenses instead of a surprise at renewal.

## How it works

Quotes come through the Premium Lock portal. You drag and drop a declarations page into the portal's AI chat window, and the quote builds itself with no manual entry. Before anything is quoted, we check eligibility against your actual policy and confirm the exact terms in writing, because a rate promise with unstated exceptions is worse than no promise at all.

## What Premium Lock is not

No product locks a rate forever. Taxes, fees, and changes you make to the policy can still move the premium. What Premium Lock addresses is the carrier-initiated increase at renewal, so the number you budgeted is the number you get.
