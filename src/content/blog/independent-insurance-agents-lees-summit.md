## What independent means

An independent agent is not tied to one insurance company. The agency holds appointments with several carriers, compares what each would charge and cover, and recommends the one that fits you. A captive agent sells for one company and can only quote that company.

Neither is wrong. The difference is how many choices you see before you decide.

## Questions to ask any agent

- **Which carriers can you quote for my type of cover?** The honest answer is rarely "all of them for everything."
- **Will you compare, or quote one company first?** You want to know whether the comparison is real.
- **Who helps me with a claim or a change after I buy?** A person you can call is worth more than a good price you cannot get answered.
- **How often will you review my policies?** Your home, cars and family change. The cover should keep up.

## What to have ready for a quote

- **Home:** the address, the year it was built, and roughly how old the roof is.
- **Auto:** each driver, and the year, make and model of each vehicle.
- **Business:** what you do, how many people work for you, and your payroll.

You do not need every detail to start. A licensed agent follows up and asks for what is missing.
