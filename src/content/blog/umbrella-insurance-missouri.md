No, Missouri does not require you to carry umbrella insurance. Whether you should buy it depends on how much you could lose if you were found responsible for a serious accident or injury. For many homeowners, the answer is yes, because it costs little compared with what it protects.

## What umbrella insurance does

An umbrella policy adds liability cover on top of the limits you already have on your home and auto policies. When a claim is larger than your auto or home liability limit, the umbrella can pay the difference, up to its own limit.

It sits on top of other policies. It does not replace them, and it is not for damage to your own house or car.

## Why the minimums are not enough

Missouri's minimum auto liability limits are $25,000 per person and $50,000 per accident for injuries, and $25,000 for property damage. A serious accident can cost far more than that. If a judgment is larger than your limits, the rest can come from your savings, your income and your home.

## Who should look at it

- **Homeowners**, because a home and savings are the first things a large claim can reach.
- **Families with teenage drivers**, because inexperienced drivers are involved in more serious accidents.
- **Anyone with a pool, a trampoline or a dog**, because injuries on your property are a common source of claims.
- **Landlords**, because tenants and visitors can bring claims against you.
- **Anyone whose income or savings would be worth going after.**

## What it costs

A personal umbrella often costs a few hundred dollars a year for $1 million of cover. The price depends on your drivers, vehicles, claims history and the limits on the policies underneath it. It is usually far cheaper per dollar of cover than raising the limits on your auto policy alone.

Most carriers expect you to carry certain minimum limits on your home and auto policies before they will add an umbrella. We check that when we quote.

## One important exception

A personal umbrella generally does not cover liability that comes from running a business. If you own a business, ask about a commercial umbrella as well.

## What to do next

When you shop your home and auto, ask for an umbrella quote in the same conversation. It is much easier to compare when the policies are quoted together, and bundling them can help with price.

**Does umbrella insurance cover my own car or house?**

No. It covers liability, meaning claims against you by other people. Your own home and car are covered by those policies.
