Usually not more, and often less. The same carrier normally charges about the same for the same cover whether you buy direct or through an agent. An independent agent can also compare several carriers, and that is where the savings tend to come from. There are exceptions, so here is how to think about it.

## Why the price is usually the same

Insurance rates are set by the carrier, and carriers pay agents out of the premium. You do not add a separate fee for the quote. So for the same carrier, the same cover and the same details, the price is generally the same direct or through an agent.

The exceptions are real. Some carriers offer online-only offers, and some policies come with fees that differ by company. When we quote, we show you the whole price, not just the headline number.

## Where an agent can save you money

- **Comparing carriers.** One carrier can be much cheaper than another for your age, address and vehicles. You would have to ask each one yourself to find out.
- **Matching cover to price.** The cheapest quote is not always the same cover. An agent lines up limits and deductibles so you compare like with like.
- **Discounts you did not know to ask for.** Bundling, safe driver, and home protection features are examples. Eligibility varies by carrier.
- **Reviews each year.** Your price can drift over time. A yearly review is the moment to check it.

## Where an agent adds value beyond price

- Someone you can call when you have a claim or a question.
- Help picking limits, not just a premium.
- Advice on what is and is not covered before you need it.

## When buying direct can make sense

If your needs are simple and you already know the carrier and the cover you want, buying direct is fine. Even then, a quick comparison costs nothing.

## Questions to ask any agent

- Which carriers can you quote for me, and for which cover?
- Is the quote the same cover and the same deductibles across carriers?
- What fees apply, and what discounts have you applied?
- Who do I call if I have a claim?
