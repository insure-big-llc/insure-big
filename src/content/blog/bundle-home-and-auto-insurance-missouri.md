Often, yes. Most carriers offer a discount when you insure your home and auto with them, and for many households the bundle is cheaper than two separate policies. It is not guaranteed, though, and the discount varies by carrier, so the right move is to compare both ways.

## Why bundling usually saves money

Carriers reward customers who put more than one policy with them, because those customers tend to stay longer and file fewer claims. The discount is usually applied to one or both policies. How large it is depends on the carrier, your details, and the cover you choose.

## When bundling may not be the best answer

- **A carrier can be right for your home and wrong for your cars.** The best price for one is not always the best for the other.
- **The discount may not make up the difference.** A bundle at one carrier can still cost more than two policies at two carriers.
- **Your cover matters more than the discount.** Do not give up limits or deductibles to chase the bundle.

## How to compare a bundle

1. **Quote the bundle** with each carrier you are considering.
2. **Quote each policy on its own** with the best carrier for that policy.
3. **Use the same limits and deductibles every time,** so you compare like with like.
4. **Add it up,** and look at the premium, the deductibles and the service together.

An independent agency can do this side by side. We quote the bundles and the separate options, and show you which is best for your situation.

## Think about umbrella insurance too

If you carry home and auto together, ask about an umbrella policy at the same time. It adds liability protection above both, and carriers often expect certain limits on the policies underneath it. Quoting everything together can make the comparison simpler.

## What to have ready

- **Home:** the address, the year it was built, and the age of the roof.
- **Auto:** each driver, and the year, make and model of each vehicle.
- Your current policies, so we can match the cover.

## Questions we have been asked

**If I bundle, does one claim affect both policies?**

Generally each policy is rated on its own claims. How a claim affects your price on the other policy depends on the carrier, so we ask before we recommend a bundle.

**Can I bundle renters or condo insurance with auto?**

Often yes. Many carriers discount renters and condo policies in the same way. We check which carriers will.
